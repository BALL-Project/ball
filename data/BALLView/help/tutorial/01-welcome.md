# Welcome to BALLView

BALLView is an interactive molecular viewer built on the BALL
biochemical algorithms library. This short tutorial walks through
the six everyday tasks: loading a structure, picking a render
style, selecting atoms, coloring, applying lighting, and saving
your work.

## What you'll see

- **3D View** in the centre — your molecule lives here.
- **Project dock** on the left — Structures, Representations, and
  Datasets in three tabs.
- **Inspector** on the right — collapsible sections to tune the
  display.
- **Bottom drawer** — Logs and the File observer.

## Getting around

- **Cmd/Ctrl + K** opens the Command palette. Search any menu by
  name. Type `?` for help, `>` for File-only matches.
- **F1** opens help for the widget under your cursor.
- Mouse: left-drag rotates, middle-drag pans, scroll zooms.

Click **Next →** to load your first structure.
