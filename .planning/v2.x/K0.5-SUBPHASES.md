## Notes for the loop driver

- Each sub-phase is one commit. Keep the same green-tests sweep at the end of each.
- K0.5.0 + K0.5.1 are the foundation — must land before .2-.5 can begin.
- K0.5.2 through K0.5.5 are roughly independent and could parallelize via worktrees, but for simplicity run them sequentially in the dynamic loop.
- K0.5.6 (benchmark) is the gate. The roadmap requires the ≥10× speedup. If the gate fails, K0.5.7 (cleanup) may need to revisit the hot path.
- After K0.5.7, queue a Codex Round 6 adversarial review before declaring K0.5 done.

## Open questions to resolve as we go

- **Variant boxing cost** (spec R2): may need `std::variant` vs custom tagged union benchmark. Defer until K0.5.6 shows a delta.
- **String pool offset stability** when atoms migrate between stores: getAtomNamePred must compare on the current store's pool, not a cached cross-store offset. Spec §4.4 covers this.
- **User-registered predicate coverage** (spec R1): K0.5.4 OwnedPred must call the v1.x predicate verbatim; verify against StandardPredicates_test.
