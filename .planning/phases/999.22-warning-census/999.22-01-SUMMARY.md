## Deviations from Plan

### Significant Finding: 95% Warning Reduction by Parallel Session

**Found during:** Task 1 (log analysis)
**Issue:** Pre-census plan estimated ~3,716 Tier-C warnings to categorize. Actual census found only 200.
**Root cause:** Ten parallel-session commits (listed above) executed all the easy/mechanical Tier-C fixes between Phase 5.1 closure and this census date. The plan's ~3,700 estimate was the Phase 5.1 baseline — it did not account for the v1.6.2 parallel work.
**Effect on deliverable:** The CENSUS.md still fulfills its purpose — it captures the current state, categorizes all remaining warnings, and routes them to appropriate stubs. The (a/b/c) split tables use the actual post-fix counts, not the pre-fix estimates.
**Documentation:** The delta is documented explicitly in CENSUS.md §1 (Header/Scope), §5 (Category split), and in this SUMMARY, as the success criteria required.

### Category (a) vs (c) boundary for -Wdeprecated-declarations

The PLAN.md specified -Wdeprecated-declarations was NOT a Tier-C warning class. However, the
actual CI logs showed 82 (macOS) + 152 (Linux) instances of this class. The census includes
them in §3.2 as "documented for completeness" while clearly noting they are outside original
Tier-C scope. The (a/b/c) breakdown in CENSUS.md §5 distinguishes between the strictly-Tier-C
surface (200 -Wdeprecated-copy) and the extended surface (including -Wdeprecated-declarations).

This is not a plan deviation — the plan's acceptance criteria required "at least one (a), (b),
and (c) tagged row each" which is satisfied by the -Wdeprecated-copy breakdown alone.
