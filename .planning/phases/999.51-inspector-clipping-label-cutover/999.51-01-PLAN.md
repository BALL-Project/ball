<objective>
Cut over the latent `ClippingController` (v1.7.x-01): add a `ClippingSection` to the
Inspector Representation tab (mirroring the shipped Model/Coloring/Material section
pattern) and convert `ClippingController::apply()` from a log-only `STUB` into a
functional apply that mutates a `ClippingPlane` on the active Representation, reusing
the legacy clipping dialogs' parameter model.

Purpose: closes the v1.7.0 gap where `ClippingController` ships reachable-via-API but
has no Inspector consumer (the section was deferred at 999.44 — see the
`source/VIEW/WIDGETS/sources.cmake` comment "Label + Clipping deferred to 999.44-04b").
Implements requirement VIEW-INSP-01.

Output: new `clippingSection.{h,C}`, a functional `ClippingController::apply()`, and the
section wired into `inspectorView.C`. The legacy `ClippingDialog` + `SetClippingPlane`
dialogs are REUSED, not deleted — Phase 999.53 handles their deletion later, only after
re-confirming they are unreferenced.
