expected: |
  `plutil -p build/.../BALLView.app/Contents/Info.plist | grep CFBundleIdentifier`

  `CFBundleName` (BALLView), `CFBundleVersion` (1.6.0), and

result: **PASS after gap fix** (2026-05-15).
