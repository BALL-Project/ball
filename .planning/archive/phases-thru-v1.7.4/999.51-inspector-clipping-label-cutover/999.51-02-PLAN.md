wired into `inspectorView.C` after Clipping. The legacy `LabelDialog` is REUSED, not deleted
— Phase 999.53 handles its deletion later, only after re-confirming it is unreferenced.

registration files (`inspectorView.{h,C}`, `source/VIEW/WIDGETS/sources.cmake`) with the
