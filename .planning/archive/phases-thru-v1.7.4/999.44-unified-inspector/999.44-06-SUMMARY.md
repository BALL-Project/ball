# Phase 999.44 Plan 06: StageController apply() cut-over Summary

**Closes the temporary scaffolding from Plan 01's exemplar.** StageController::apply() now mutates the live Stage; the read-only-mirror stub + warning Log line are gone.

## What changed

`StageController::apply()` migrated from a `Log.info() << "STUB"` body to actual mutation calls on the Stage:
- backgroundColor → `Stage::setBackgroundColor(ColorRGBA::set(QColor))`
- showCoordinateSystem → `Stage::showCoordinateSystem(bool)`
- fogIntensity → `Stage::setFogIntensity(float)`
- eyeDistance → `Stage::setEyeDistance(float)`
- focalDistance → `Stage::setFocalDistance(float)`

## Consistency-by-construction invariant achieved

For the migrated fields: legacy StageSettings dialog + Inspector StageSection + Inspector BackgroundSection **all route mutation through `StageController::apply()`**. This is the Handover §risks core invariant.

(Fields the controller does NOT yet mirror — vertex-buffer toggle, downsampling, animation smoothness — remain owned by the legacy StageSettings::apply() body until additional Q_PROPERTYs land on the controller.)
