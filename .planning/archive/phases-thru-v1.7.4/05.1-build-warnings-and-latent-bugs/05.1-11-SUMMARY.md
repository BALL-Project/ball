## Deviations from Plan

### Scope-bound discovery — deferred to follow-up

**1. [Scope discovery] `actions/upload-artifact@v4` and `actions/download-artifact@v4` are also Node-20 internally**
- **Found during:** Pre-flight `gh api` audit of all `@v4` action pins in the workflow files.
- **Issue:** `actions/upload-artifact@v4` (5 instances in ci.yml + release.yml) and `actions/download-artifact@v4` (1 instance in release.yml) declare `using: 'node20'` in their `action.yml`. They will eventually emit the same Node-20-deprecation annotation when GitHub broadens the annotation surface (currently the annotation engine names only checkout + cache).
- **Why deferred:**
  1. NOT in today's annotation surface — bumping them is preemptive, not corrective.
  2. The Node-24-native versions are `upload-artifact@v6` and `download-artifact@v7` — both jump TWO majors past v4 each, and v5 of both introduced **breaking behavioral changes** (single-artifact-naming semantics, compression defaults). Bumping them inside this plan would inflate "trivial diff" into a behavioral-risk change immediately ahead of the v1.6.0 release path.
  3. The plan's `<action>` instruction explicitly allows recording deferred bumps in SUMMARY.
- **Recommended:** add a new BACKLOG entry (or fold into D2) and resolve under a dedicated plan once v1.6.0 has shipped. Defaults to consider:
  - `upload-artifact@v6` — first Node-24-native version; minimal jump.
  - `download-artifact@v7` — first Node-24-native version; minimal jump.
- **Not committed in this plan.**
