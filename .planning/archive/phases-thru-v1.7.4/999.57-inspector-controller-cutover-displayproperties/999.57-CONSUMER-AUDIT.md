## Phase-split note for 999.53 (scene.C dialogs are NOT in this phase)

This phase (999.57) detaches ONLY the **model + coloring** render-path dialog triples
(`displayProperties` / `modelSettingsDialog` / `coloringSettingsDialog`). The
`scene.C` **stage / light / material** dialogs are a SEPARATE concern and are NOT covered here —
they need the recommended sibling phase (a stage/light/material controller extraction analogous to
Plans 01/02) BEFORE 999.53 can delete them. 999.53's class-C worklist above is scoped to the two
render-path triples only; do not fold the scene dialogs into it.
