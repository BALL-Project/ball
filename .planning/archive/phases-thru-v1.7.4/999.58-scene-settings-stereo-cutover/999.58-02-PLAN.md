<objective>
Relocate the full NON-STEREO stage render configuration that `scene.C` reaches through `StageSettings::apply()` into the existing Inspector `StageController`, then route `Scene::applyPreferences()`'s stage branch through the controller instead of `stage_settings_->apply()`. Also relocate (or prove non-load-bearing) the `StageSettings::getGLSettings()` capability readback that runs at preferences-tab init.

This is the SECOND of three cut-over plans. The 999.44 cut-over already migrated background/coordinate-system/fog/eye-distance/focal-distance onto StageController (the exemplar). This plan completes the field coverage for the ~10 render-affecting fields the controller does NOT yet mirror (vertex buffers, smooth lines, downsampling, projection mode, FPS, preview, offscreen, mouse/wheel sensitivity, show-light-sources, animation smoothness, capping color, OpenGL renderer switch) and switches `applyPreferences` over. It does NOT remove the `stage_settings_` construction at scene.C:162 or the Preferences-stack registration — Plan 03 does that after the stereo path is handled.

Purpose: VIEW-CLEAN-03 requires scene.C to no longer depend on StageSettings for driving the rendered scene. StageSettings::apply() is the single largest live render-config consumer; relocating it is the bulk of the stage cut-over.
Output: StageController owning the complete non-stereo stage render config; scene.C applyPreferences stage branch + the getGLSettings init call routed off stage_settings_.
</objective>
