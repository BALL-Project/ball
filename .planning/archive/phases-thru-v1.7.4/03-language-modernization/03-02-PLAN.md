raw `-std=` flags and the obsolete deprecation-warning suppression.

`-std=` flags. This is also what *surfaces* the C++17 breakage that Plan 03's
build-and-fix loop resolves — so this plan is one of Plan 03's two prerequisites.
Output: `CMakeLists.txt` and `cmake/BALLCompilerSpecific.cmake` modified; the build is
configured as strict C++17 with no raw `-std=` anywhere under `cmake/`.
</objective>
